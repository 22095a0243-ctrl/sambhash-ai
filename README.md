# Sambhash AI — Public SaaS Landing Page

A premium, modern, responsive public-facing marketing and product presentation website for **Sambhash AI** ([https://SambhashAi.com](https://sambhashai.com/)), an AI voice-calling and business automation SaaS platform.

---

## 🚀 Key Highlights & Sections

1. **Navigation Bar**: Responsive header with brand identity, smooth scroll links (`Features`, `How It Works`, `Preview`, `Use Cases`, `Pricing`, `FAQ`), and entry points to `Log In` and `Get Started`.
2. **Hero Section**: 
   - Tagline: *"Turn Every Conversation Into an Opportunity."*
   - Clear value proposition & primary/secondary CTAs.
   - High-fidelity product interface preview with interactive agent status, active call duration, waveform visualizer, and illustrative lead pipeline.
3. **Product Capabilities (6-Grid)**:
   - AI Voice Agents
   - Lead Management
   - Calling Campaigns
   - Call Intelligence
   - Workflow Automation
   - Usage and Billing
4. **How It Works**: 4-step structured process (`Add Leads` ➔ `Configure Agent` ➔ `Launch Campaign` ➔ `Review Results`) with compliance & telecom notices.
5. **Interactive Product Preview**: Realistic tabbed dashboard simulation (`Overview`, `AI Agents`, `Leads`, `Campaigns`, `Calls & Intelligence`, `Wallet & Usage`).
6. **Industry Use Cases**: Real Estate, Coaching Centres, Consultancies, and Sales & Support Teams.
7. **Integrations**: Connectivity overview featuring n8n, CRM platforms, WhatsApp, spreadsheets, speech providers (Sarvam AI / OmniDimension), and Razorpay billing.
8. **Transparent Usage-Based Pricing**:
   - **Basic**: ₹3.50 / min
   - **Standard**: ₹5.00 / min
   - **Premium**: ₹7.00 / min
   - Clearly documented tax details & wallet drawdown terms.
9. **Trust & Reliability**: Transparent usage tracking, operational visibility, role-based controls, and credential encryption.
10. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**: 8 accordion answers covering agents, imports, bulk calling, recordings, and billing.
11. **Final Call to Action**: Clear entry points to start or sign in.
12. **Footer & Legal Routes**: Dynamic copyright year, support email (`support@sambhashai.com`), along with dedicated `Privacy Policy` (`/privacy`) and `Terms of Service` (`/terms`) pages.
13. **Auth Entry Routes**: Dedicated `/login` and `/signup` pages matching the Sambhash AI design system.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Framework**: Next.js 14 (App Router)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with custom palette (slate charcoal, soft off-white background `#FAFAFC`, indigo brand accent `#4F46E5`)
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Typography**: Inter / system clean sans-serif
- **Accessibility**: Reduced motion query support, semantic HTML5, aria attributes
